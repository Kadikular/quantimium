# Progression

*Every chapter is paid for with something the chapter before taught you to make.*

Quantimium unfolds in seven chapters. Each ends with a material only that chapter gives, and the next chapter's
machines are built from it. If you're stuck, look at the end of the chapter you're in: that's what you're missing.
New to the mod? Start with [Getting started](getting-started.md). The [machine tiers](../concepts/tiers.md) page
lists every machine by tier.

| Chapter | You'll | It ends with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. Observation](#1-observation) | Collapse strange matter into the mod's first material | [[item:quantimium_trace]] |
| [2. The Field](#2-the-field) | Heat the field, and pull its anomaly back down | [[item:anomaly_fragment]] |
| [3. The Mirror](#3-the-mirror) | Step into the mirror and harvest what grows on your machines | [[item:anomalite_shard]] |
| [4. Rifts](#4-rifts) | Grow a rift, then close it from the mirror | [[item:rift_residue]] |
| [5. The Foundry](#5-the-foundry) | Build the multiblocks, and run a base hot | [[item:tesseract]] |
| [6. ||6:The Veiled||](#6-the-veiled) | ||6:Take the thing that comes through, and hold it|| | ||6:[[item:veil_thread]]|| |
| [7. ||7:The Reactor||](#7-the-reactor) | ||7:Hold everything in one horizon|| | |

[Lasers](#lasers-alongside) run alongside from chapter 1.

## 1. Observation

Mine [[block:unrealised_ore]]. It looks like ordinary ore until it's mined, and drops [[item:unrealised_matter]].
An [[block:observation_chamber]] collapses Matter into ordinary items on a redstone pulse, and now and then leaves a
[[item:quantimium_trace]].

**Milestones**

- Mine Unrealized Matter.
- Collapse it in an Observation Chamber.
- Find a Quantimium Trace.
- Make a [[item:flux_meter]] and read your chunk.
- Build a [[block:basic_quantum_crafter]] from a [[item:basic_decoherence_matrix]].

| Stuck? | Why | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No Traces | The chamber works on a pulse, and leaves a Trace only some of the time | Give it a redstone clock and keep it fed |

## 2. The Field

Every craft adds [flux](../concepts/flux-and-anomaly.md) to the chunks around it, and anomaly follows. Flux unlocks
recipes; anomaly is the cost. A [[block:basic_anomaly_siphon]] pulls anomaly down and turns it into
[[item:anomaly_fragment]]s, the material for everything after.

**Milestones**

- Raise your field to Medium: run machines, or a [[block:quantum_exciter]].
- Place a Basic Anomaly Siphon.
- Collect an Anomaly Fragment.

| Stuck? | Why | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No Anomaly Fragments | A siphon only gets what it pulls: no anomaly, no fragments | Heat the field with machines or an Exciter |
| The field won't climb | Small machines add little | An Exciter heats it on purpose |

## 3. The Mirror

The world has a gray copy, the [mirror](../mirror/mirror-phase.md). From **Medium anomaly**, in a chunk that isn't
contained, tears open into it on their own, and [[block:anomalite_crystal]]s start growing on your powered
machines. You can see and break the crystals only from the mirror, and they grow fully only at **High** anomaly. A
grown crystal breaks into [[item:anomalite_shard]]s.

**Milestones**

- Walk through a tear into the mirror.
- See a crystal on one of your machines.
- Break a grown crystal for shards.
- Make a [[item:mirror_lens]], and see a crystal's ink from the real world.
- Build a [[block:quantum_crafter]] or [[block:quantum_simulator]].
- Make a [[item:decoherence_lance]].

| Stuck? | Why | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No crystals | They need Medium anomaly, an uncontained chunk, and a powered machine | Check with the Flux Meter; turn off containment nearby |
| I can't see them | They're only visible from the mirror | Walk into a tear |
| They stay small | They grow fully only at High anomaly | Heat the field further |
| No tears | Tears open only at Medium anomaly, uncontained | Heat the field |

## 4. Rifts

A [flux rift](../mirror/flux-rifts.md) is a tear that failed to close. It grows, leaks anomaly and throws lightning,
and only a Decoherence Lance closes it, from the mirror. Close it once it's grown and it leaves
[[item:rift_residue]], the material for the High tier.

**Milestones**

- Find a rift, or plant a [[item:rift_seed]] in an uncontained chunk at Medium anomaly or more.
- Watch it grow to stage 2.
- Build a [[block:stabilised_portal_frame]] portal, a way in and out you choose.
- Close a rift with the Lance.
- Collect Rift Residue.

| Stuck? | Why | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The seed fizzles | It needs Medium anomaly or more where it lands, uncontained | Use it somewhere hotter |
| Closing gave nothing | A stage 1 rift gives no residue | Let it grow to stage 2 first |
| It won't grow | A contained chunk, or Low anomaly, freezes it | Keep its chunk hot and uncontained |

## 5. The Foundry

Residue builds the [[block:quantum_foundry_controller]], which grows what no table can: the [[item:tesseract]]
among it. Then come the [Containment Hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md), to run a base hot without the
anomaly, and a [stabilized rift](../mirror/stabilised-rift.md) for a steady supply of residue.

**Milestones**

- Form a Quantum Foundry.
- Grow a Tesseract.
- Form an [[block:anomaly_containment_hall]].
- Hold a rift with a [[block:rift_anchor]] and [[block:rift_stabiliser]]s.
- Fold a machine in a [Fold Chamber](../multiblocks/fold-chamber.md).
- Step into a [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md).

| Stuck? | Why | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Foundry waits | It needs a flux band, flux in its chunk, power and enough arms | Read its status line |
| A Crafter ignores its catalyst | Some catalysts need a hotter band | The Crafter's screen says which band |

## 6. ||6:The Veiled|| { data-toc-label="6. Spoiler" }

<div class="spoiler-block" data-spoiler="6" markdown>

The worst rifts let something through: [the Veiled](../mirror/veiled.md). It can't be killed, only driven off and
held. Held in a Containment Hall, it gives up [[item:veil_thread]].

**Milestones**

- See a sighting of the Veiled.
- Let a wild rift grow to stage 4 in a chunk at **Critical flux**, uncontained: it can release one.
- Drive it off with the Lance.
- Hold it in a powered Containment Hall stocked with residue.
- Draw Veil Thread from it: a laser with a Pickup Lens through the hall's glass.

| Stuck? | Why | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No Veiled | Only a stage 4 wild rift at Critical flux releases one | Let a rift grow uncontained in a Critical field |
| It got away | The hall had no residue, or was out of reach | Stock the hall, and fight it close by |

</div>

## 7. ||7:The Reactor|| { data-toc-label="7. Spoiler" }

<div class="spoiler-block" data-spoiler="7" markdown>

Veil Thread builds the [[block:laser_emitter_3]] and the [[block:ring_emitter]]s. The
[Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md) holds everything in one horizon and makes anything its catalysts can.
It needs a Singularity, folded from a Tesseract in an empty Fold Chamber, and works without rings; each pair of
Ring Emitters makes its horizon bigger.

**Milestones**

- Fold a Singularity.
- Form a Reactor.
- Light a ring.

</div>

## Lasers alongside

[Lasers](../lasers/overview.md) start in chapter 1 and grow with you:

- **Chapter 1:** a [[block:flash_emitter]] burns coal into a laser. A [[item:laser_focus]] is copper and glass, and
  [[block:laser_port]]s, [[block:laser_tank]]s and a [[block:logistics_controller]] need nothing more.
- **Chapter 3:** shards build the [[block:laser_emitter_1]], the [[block:lens_mount]] and lenses that mine, fight
  and move items.
- **Chapter 5:** the [[block:laser_emitter_2]].
- **Chapter 7:** ||7:the [[block:laser_emitter_3]].||
