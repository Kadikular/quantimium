# Getting started

The first hour of Quantimium, from the first strange ore to your first crafter.

## 1. Unrealized Ore

[[block:unrealised_ore]] looks like ordinary stone or deepslate ore until you mine it. It drops [[item:unrealised_matter]]. Fortune gives more. Silk Touch keeps the ore itself, which machines take as Matter too.

With [GuideME](https://modrinth.com/mod/guideme) installed, a Book and one Unrealized Matter make the **Quantimium Field Guide**: this wiki, in the game.

## 2. Observing it

An [[block:observation_chamber]] collapses Unrealized Matter into ordinary items on a redstone pulse. Now and then it leaves a [[item:quantimium_trace]], the mod's first real material. Later, mites killed in the [mirror](../mirror/mirror-phase.md) leave Traces too.

## 3. The first machines

- **[[item:flux_meter]]:** reads the flux and anomaly of the chunk you stand in. Make one early.
- **[[item:basic_decoherence_matrix]]:** an ender eye held in four Traces. It is the core of the **[[block:basic_quantum_crafter]]**, which runs table, furnace and stonecutter recipes.
- **[[block:flux_detector]]:** turns your chunk's band into a redstone signal.

## 4. Power and the field

From here machines run on FE. Every craft adds [flux](../concepts/flux-and-anomaly.md) to the chunks around it, and anomaly follows. Four blocks keep it in check:

- **[[block:quantum_exciter]]:** raises flux when you need a higher band.
- **[[block:basic_anomaly_siphon]]:** keeps anomaly down around a small workshop, and turns what it pulls into [[item:anomaly_fragment]]s.
- **[[block:flux_suppressor]]:** holds flux down where you want none: a firebreak around a hot base, or near a rift.
- **[Field control](../field-control/overview.md):** the stronger versions, for later.

## 5. What comes next

- The [[block:quantum_crafter]] and [[block:quantum_simulator]] take over your crafting.
- [Anomalite](../mirror/anomalite.md) crystals start growing on your machines once anomaly runs Medium (a few; more at High). They are a hazard and a resource.
- Then come the [Quantum Foundry](../multiblocks/quantum-foundry.md), the [mirror](../mirror/mirror-phase.md), [rifts](../mirror/flux-rifts.md) and [the Veiled](../mirror/veiled.md).
- [Lasers](../lasers/overview.md) carry power across a base and, with lenses, mine, fight and move items.
- At the end of it all stands the [Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md).
