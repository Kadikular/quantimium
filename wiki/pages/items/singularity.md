A Singularity is a Tesseract collapsed into a black hole. It is the horizon of a [Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md), and holds everything the Reactor holds.

## How to get it

Put an **unbound** [[item:tesseract]] in the core of an **empty** [Fold Chamber](../multiblocks/fold-chamber.md) at least {{c:Folding.SINGULARITY_MIN_SIDE}} blocks every way and press Fold. With nothing inside to hold the volume open it collapses, for {{c:Folding.SINGULARITY_FE}} FE.

## Using it

- Seat it in a [[block:horizon_core]] by using it on the core. It carries everything with it when you take it out.
- A dropped Singularity never despawns and doesn't burn, in fire or lava. Only the void takes it.
- Carrying one that holds something earns *Should you be holding that?*
- Its tooltip says it is safe.
