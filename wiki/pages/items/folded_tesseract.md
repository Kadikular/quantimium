A Folded Tesseract is a whole multiblock folded into one item, made in a [Fold Chamber](../multiblocks/fold-chamber.md).

- Its tooltip says what it holds, the box it was folded from, and how much room it needs to unfold.
- **As a catalyst:** in a [Quantum Crafter](../machines/quantum-crafter.md) it runs every recipe the machine does, at a Critical field. A folded Foundry makes only the recipes its arms allow.
- **Unfolding:** in any Fold Chamber with room for it, with every cell it needs empty. It stands up the way it was built, and you get the Tesseract back bound to the controller.
- Contents never cross a fold, and a folded machine can't tick or be touched.
