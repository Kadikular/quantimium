The Cavity Mirror is a Laser Focus attachment: a flat half-silvered plate on one face, facing out. A laser out of it that comes back to it is held there. A cavity is a battery for a laser network.

- Each Cavity Mirror holds {{c:LaserTuning.CAVITY_FE_PER_BLOCK}} FE per block of gap and takes or gives up to {{c:LaserTuning.CAVITY_RATE}} FE/t. The gap is up to {{c:LaserTuning.CAVITY_MAX_GAP}} blocks.
- It needs a flat [[block:laser_mirror]] facing it, or another Cavity Mirror. Mirrors in between are fine; a splitter breaks it.
- What it holds leaves by the focus's Emitters. A cavity keeps what its laser does, so a Harvest cavity gives out Harvest.

There is also a standalone Cavity Mirror block with the same job. See [Optical cavities](../lasers/cavities.md).
