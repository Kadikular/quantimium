Reads the field. Use it in the air for the chunk you stand in, or on a block for that block's chunk:
its [flux and anomaly](../concepts/flux-and-anomaly.md), each with its band, on the action bar.

[[mechanic:flux_meter.readouts]] Used on a block that has something to say, it adds a line about it:

| Block | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| [[block:quantum_crafter]], [[block:quantum_simulator]], [[block:quantum_observation_chamber]], [[block:quantum_foundry_controller]] (or any Foundry part), ME Superposition Crafter | Its average FE/t, and the flux a second that puts into this chunk |
| [[block:quantum_exciter]] | The same, at its ten-fold flux |
| [[block:zeno_field_controller]] | Holding or accelerating, its radius and factor, then its FE/t and flux |
| [[block:rift_anchor]] | The rift's stage, how many stabilisers hold it and the residue an hour; or that it is loose, or that there is no rift |
| [[block:rift_stabiliser]] | Beaming at a rift (and at what FE/t), claimed but idle, or free |
| [[block:decoherence_projector]] | Firing, watching, or out of power, and its FE/t |
| [[block:harvest_laser]] | Firing (its FE/t and how near the next shard is), or what's wrong with its line, and the shards it holds |
| [[block:flux_suppressor]], [[block:basic_anomaly_siphon]], and the other field control blocks | Its setpoints and its FE/t |
| [[block:anomaly_containment_hall]] | Its FE/t, its pull and its rating |
| [[block:budding_anomalite]], [[block:anomalite_crystal]] | What it feeds on, and a crystal's stage |
| [[block:flux_detector]] | What it watches and the signal it gives |
| [[block:entangled_dock]] | The item it is charging, and at what FE/t |
| [[block:flux_maintainer]], [[block:anomaly_siphon]], [[block:field_regulator]] | Its floor and ceiling, and its FE/t |
| [[block:superposition_pod]] | Its name, whose double it holds (or that it is empty), and its stored FE |
| [[block:unfolding_array]] | How far along it is with unfolding someone, or that it is idle |

The flux a machine puts into its own chunk is a fifth of what it emits: the rest goes to the eight chunks round it.
