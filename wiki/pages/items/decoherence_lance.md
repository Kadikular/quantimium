The one tool that reaches into the rift. Held from the [mirror](../concepts/mirror-phase.md), its beam
[closes flux rifts](../mechanics/flux-rifts.md#closing-it), unmakes mirror mites, and
[wears down the Veiled](../mechanics/veiled.md#the-lance-aggravating-it).

It runs on FE, holding {{c:DecoherenceLanceItem.CAPACITY}}: charge it in any mod's item charger, or keep it
topped up from an [[block:entangled_dock]].
