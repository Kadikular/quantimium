The Laser Focus is where lasers gather and set off again. Put it down, then put attachments on its faces, one to a face.

- A laser reaching it by any face without a Stand or Cover stops there and leaves by each Emitter, shared by what each laser's blocks ask for.
- Sneak-click it (by hand or with a wrench) to take the focus out and leave the attachments.
- Without a Focus Stand it floats.
