A prism of Anomalite shards sealed in glass: it turns ordinary light into light that reaches into
the mirror, and cracks as it does. It's what a [[block:decoherence_projector]] burns to beam at the
Veiled, mites and rifts, and it's used up whole. Made in the [Quantum Foundry](../multiblocks/quantum-foundry.md)
at Medium flux from two Anomalite shards, a little iron and glass.
