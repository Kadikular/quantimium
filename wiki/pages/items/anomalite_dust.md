Anomalite Dust is what a crystal broken before it is grown leaves, and what a crushed shard becomes.

- The [[block:quantum_foundry_controller]] refines it back into a [[item:anomalite_shard]].
- The [[block:materialiser]] is paid in it. A [[item:crushing_lens]] can turn shards into dust for that.
