Isolator Lens lets power through, and only what else it is set to pass.

## How it works

- Unset, it passes power only, as the [[block:isolator]] does: the laser leaving it carries nothing it did (actions, Pickup, UV), and nothing comes back up through it, so Pickup's items stop short of it.
- The laser's reach always passes.
- An Isolator alone in a crafting grid makes one.

## Settings

Use the lens in the air to set it.

- **Power**: on by default. Turn it off and nothing past the lens is charged by its laser, though what the laser does past it is still paid for.
- **Logistics network**, **Actions**, **Modifiers** and **Redstone** (a Redstone Lens's signal): switch on to let them pass.
- **List** of lenses: allow lets through only those, deny stops just those. For example, Actions on with Harvest denied passes every action but Harvest.
