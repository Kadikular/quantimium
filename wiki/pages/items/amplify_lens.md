[[mechanic:laser.lens.amplify]] Amplify Lens strengthens the actions before it. Up to {{c:LaserTuning.AMPLIFY_LENSES}} count.

## How it works

- **Harvest** mines as a diamond pickaxe, so obsidian and the like can be cut. Each lens adds one share to a cut's cost.
- **Damage** hits twice as hard per lens, for twice the cost.
- **Healing** heals twice as much per lens, for twice the cost.

## Energy

Free itself. It raises the cost of Harvest, Damage and Healing: see [Lens energy](../lasers/lenses/energy.md).
