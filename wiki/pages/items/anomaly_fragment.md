Anomaly Fragments are produced by the Anomaly Siphons as they hold anomaly down: the [[block:basic_anomaly_siphon]], the [[block:anomaly_siphon]] and the [[block:field_regulator]].

- One fragment per {{c:FieldControlBlockEntity.ANOMALY_PER_FRAGMENT}} anomaly pulled, into a slot pipes and hoppers can take from. A Siphon in a Quantum Simulator makes them a batch at a time.
- A common ingredient in Mid-tier machines, and in the Superposition Pod's modules.
