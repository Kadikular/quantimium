[[mechanic:laser.lens.speed]] Speed Lens makes the actions before it work twice as fast. Three count, for eight times.

## How it works

- Harvest waits half as long between blocks, and Placement and Interact act twice as often.
- Healing sweeps more often, up to four times.
- Insert and Extract move twice as many items and wait half as long.
- Acceleration doubles its speed-up: 4x, 8x, 16x.
- An [[block:area_prism]] looks through its area twice as fast.
- Damage is not changed.

## Energy

Free itself. It raises how much the actions spend each second.

## Applies to

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| [[item:harvest_lens]] | Halves the wait between blocks, once per lens (10 ticks at base). |
| [[item:damage_lens]] | No effect. |
| [[item:healing_lens]] | Sweeps twice as often per lens, up to four times (every 10, 5, then 2 ticks). |
| [[item:freezing_lens]] | Through an Area Prism, looks at more blocks each sweep. |
| [[item:interact_lens]] | Acts twice as often per lens (10 ticks at base). |
| [[item:acceleration_lens]] | Each lens doubles the speed-up: 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x (the config sets the most). |
| [[item:place_lens]] | Places twice as often per lens (10 ticks at base). |
| [[item:insert_lens]] | Moves 8 items per 10 ticks at base; each lens doubles that (16, 32, 64) and halves the wait. |
| [[item:extract_lens]] | Moves 8 items per 10 ticks at base; each lens doubles that (16, 32, 64) and halves the wait. |
| [[item:redstone_lens]] | No effect. |
