An Anomalite Shard is what a fully grown [[block:anomalite_crystal]] breaks into: 3 shards. See [Anomalite](../mirror/anomalite.md) for how to harvest a crystal.

Shards also come from [[item:anomalite_dust]] (refined in the Foundry) and from an [[block:anomalite_lattice]]. They make lattices, cells and emitters.
