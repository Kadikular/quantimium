A strand of the Veiled, and the only way to get one is to draw it from a Veiled held in an [Anomaly Containment Hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md).

## How to get it

1. **Hold a Veiled.** Wear one down and let a powered, fed Containment Hall take it (see [Ending a fight](../mirror/veiled.md#ending-a-fight)).
2. **Aim a laser at the cell.** Point a laser through the middle of any wall of the hall's glass. A [[block:rift_lens]] against the glass seats onto it with a studded collar, so the laser reaches the cell from the mirror's side.
3. **Add a Pickup Lens.** The laser needs a [[item:pickup_lens]] on it. It is only the mirror's Pickup that draws thread.
4. **Wait.** It draws one thread every {{c:MirrorHarvest.THREAD_TICKS|min}} at {{c:MirrorHarvest.THREAD_FE_PER_TICK}} FE/t. One draw to a hall at a time. The thread goes up the laser to a collecting [[block:laser_port]].

See [Networks and rules](../lasers/networks.md#into-the-mirror) for how a laser behaves in the mirror.

## Uses

Veil Thread goes into the top laser parts: the [[block:laser_emitter_3]] and the [[block:ring_emitter]].
