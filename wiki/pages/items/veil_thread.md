A strand of the Veiled itself. A [[block:harvest_laser]] draws one out of a Veiled held in an
[Anomaly Containment Hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md#veil-thread), through a [[block:rift_lens]]
and the hall's glass, every three minutes. It's the only way to get it.

It goes into upgrades and capstones, not everyday recipes; nothing uses it yet.
