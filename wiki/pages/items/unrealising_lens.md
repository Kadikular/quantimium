[[mechanic:laser.lens.unrealise]] Unrealizing Lens makes ore a Harvest laser cuts (anything in `#c:ores`) come away as [[item:unrealised_matter]], one per block, with no history yet.

## How it works

- A pack can turn it off in the config, where Matter would duplicate ores.

## Energy

Free.
