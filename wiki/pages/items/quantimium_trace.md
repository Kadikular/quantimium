Quantimium Trace is the residue of measuring something into existence. Most low-tier Quantimium machines are built from it.

## How to get it

- **Observation Chambers.** Each collapse has a {{c:ObservationChamberBlockEntity.TRACE_CHANCE|pct}} chance of leaving a Trace.
- **Processed Matter.** A Chamber that stops partway through processed Matter's history leaves a Trace.
- **Mirror mites.** One killed by a player leaves a Trace a quarter of the time, in the mirror.
