The Laser Linker links a Laser Prism to the blocks its laser should reach, and sets the area of an Area Prism.

- Use it on a prism to pick it, then on each block its laser should reach (again to unlink). Sneak-use in the air to let the prism go.
- Use it in the air to switch to area mode, then use it on two corners, then on the prism. The area shows as you pick it.
- Holding it shows every laser faintly, even ones carrying nothing, and the area of any prism you look at.
- It shows a UV Lens's hidden laser.

See [Prisms](../lasers/prisms.md).
