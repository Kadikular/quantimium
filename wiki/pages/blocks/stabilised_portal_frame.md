The Stabilized Portal Frame builds a way into the mirror and back.

- Build a 4×5 frame of it with a 2×3 opening inside, then light it with a [[item:rift_seed]]: a rift opened on purpose and held in the frame.
- The portal works from both sides.
- Phased players use it as a way in or out of [the mirror](../mirror/mirror-phase.md).
