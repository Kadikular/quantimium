A [[block:superposition_pod]] module, set in one of the four edges of its [[block:pod_cradle]]. It works while the pod holds one of your doubles.

Keeps hostile mobs and the Veiled off the double. Without a Ward, an unguarded double is knocked out by a mob at the glass after {{c:SuperpositionPodBlockEntity.KNOCK_OUT_STRIKES}} seconds.

Draw: {{c:SuperpositionPodBlockEntity.WARD_FE_PER_TICK}} FE/t.

See the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md#modules) page.
