Budding Anomalite grows [[block:anomalite_crystal]]s in the mirror while it is powered. It ignores flux, anomaly and containment, so you can farm in a quiet chunk. It pays a flat FE/t, and its crystals never drain another machine, shrink, or count against the chunk's crystal cap.

Craft it from gold ingots, a lattice, shards and a block of amethyst. See [Anomalite](../mirror/anomalite.md#from-a-budding-anomalite).
