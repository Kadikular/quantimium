The Logistics Controller starts a logistics network. A laser in by its back goes on out of its outputs, with a laser of its own. Everything that laser reaches is one network.

- One controller to a network on each channel: where lasers from two on the same channel meet, both stop and say why.
- Right-click it for its screen: how many storage, provider and requester receptacles it has, its power, and its
  [channel](../lasers/channels.md) on a tab. Sneak-right-click a face to switch it as an output.
- It has its own store ({{c:LaserTuning.LOGISTICS_CAPACITY}} FE) and pays {{c:LaserTuning.LOGISTICS_FE_PER_MOVE}} FE for each move its receptacles make. It gives no power to machines.
- Lenses past it use the network. Pickup and Extract put items into it; Insert, Placement and Interact (through an Filter Lens) take items from it.

See [Logistics](../lasers/logistics.md).
