A stone ring laser light passes through into the mirror (see
[Into the mirror](../lasers/networks.md#into-the-mirror)). On its own it does nothing. With lit light going
through it along its axis, a small tear opens in its middle and the lens lights up. Everyone can see
the tear, from either side of the mirror.

Placed against a hall's glass it faces into the glass, ready to seat on it; anywhere else it faces the
way you do, unless that would point it into the block you placed it against. Use any wrench on it to
turn it to the next axis.

[[mechanic:rift_lens.struts]] **It holds on to what's beside it.** A strut grows from the lens to any
solid face next to it in its own plane, so it stands on a floor, hangs from a ceiling or sits on a
wall. Lenses turned the same way hold on to each other too, so they stack.

**Seated.** Right against a hall's glass, the lens seats onto it with a short barrel and a studded
collar. It's only a look: a lens anywhere on the line works the same.
