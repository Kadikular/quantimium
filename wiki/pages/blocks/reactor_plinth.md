The Reactor Plinth is the disc the [Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md) stands on: 11 blocks across with its corners rounded off, the [[block:horizon_core]] at the center.

- Its circuit traces run on from block to block wherever it is built, and light up when the Reactor forms.
- While the Reactor works they carry signals. Idle, they glow still.
- Ring Emitters, Catalyst Bays and ports replace plinth blocks.
