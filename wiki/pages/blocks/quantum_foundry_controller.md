The Quantum Foundry controller is the heart of the [Quantum Foundry](../multiblocks/quantum-foundry.md), a timed crafter that spends FE and local flux to make the field's own materials.

- Build a 3×3 ring of [[block:quantum_foundry_plinth]] around it, with one to four attunement arms (conduit, pillar and tank) out of the ring's sides.
- Put an ingredient on each pillar. Recipes name the lowest flux band they work in, the flux they spend, and the arms they need.
- It stores {{c:QuantumFoundryBlockEntity.ENERGY_CAPACITY}} FE and takes up to {{c:QuantumFoundryBlockEntity.MAX_RECEIVE}} FE/t, through any plinth block.

See the [Quantum Foundry](../multiblocks/quantum-foundry.md) page for recipes and what each status means.
