The Pod Cradle is the base a [[block:superposition_pod]] stands on. A 3×3 cradle sits one block below the capsule: Pod Cradle at the four corners and under the capsule, and Pod Plating or a module on each of the four edges. A whole cradle lights up, and take a block out and the pod stops working until it is back. Power goes in at the capsule or through any block of the cradle.
