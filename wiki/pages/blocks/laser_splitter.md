The Laser Splitter is a Laser Mirror with a half-silvered plate. A laser reaching it goes straight on and is reflected, off both sides of the plate: one laser becomes two, sharing the power by what each end asks for.

- Set the plate the same way as a Laser Mirror.
- Two lasers through one splitter stay apart.
- A laser never leaves through its stand.
- A splitter closes no optical cavity.

See [Mirrors and splitters](../lasers/mirrors.md#laser-splitter).
