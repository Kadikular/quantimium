The Laser Tank is a laser's fluid end, as the [[block:laser_port]] is its item end. It passes a laser through (in by its back, out of its face) and holds {{c:LaserTankBlockEntity.TANKS}} tanks of {{c:LaserTankBlockEntity.CAPACITY}} mB, one fluid in each.

- **Collect** (the default): catches what an Extract Lens drains, for pipes to take away.
- **Send**: sends what it holds down the laser, to an Insert Lens or another Laser Tank further along.
- **Both**: catches, then sends down.
- Use a bucket on it to fill or empty it. Pipes drain it on any face, and fill it only while it sends.

See [Laser Ports](../lasers/ports.md#laser-tanks).
