A [[block:superposition_pod]] module, set in one of the four edges of its [[block:pod_cradle]]. It works while the pod holds one of your doubles.

A [[block:logistics_terminal]] in the pod's cradle. Point a Logistics Controller's laser at it, and the logistics key (V by default) opens that network's terminal from anywhere while the pod holds one of your doubles.

Draw: as the controller pays.

See the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md#modules) page.
