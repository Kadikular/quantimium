The Solar Emitter turns sunlight into a laser through its glass. It needs no power and holds none.

- Makes {{c:LaserTuning.SOLAR_FE}} FE/t in full sun. Dawn, dusk and rain give less; night and a roof give none.
- Lasers that reach it join its own and leave by its emitter, up to {{c:LaserTuning.SOLAR_CHAIN_MAX}} FE/t in all. A row of eight in full sun gives as much as a row can.
- Reach: {{c:LaserTuning.SOLAR_RANGE}} blocks per run.
- Placed, it shines toward you. Sneak-click a side to send its laser there. It can't shine up, through its glass.

See [Emitters](../lasers/emitters.md#solar-emitter).
