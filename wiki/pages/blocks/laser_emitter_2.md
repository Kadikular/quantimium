The Laser Emitter II turns power into a laser. It takes FE on any face and sends it out of its outputs, as much as the blocks the laser reaches ask for, up to 32,000 FE/t.

- Reach: 32 blocks per run. Buffer: 3,200,000 FE. Ready cost: 16 FE/t.
- Placed, it faces you, and its laser comes out toward you. Sneak-click a face with an empty hand, or use a wrench, to add or remove an output. One emitter can shine from all six faces, and always keeps at least one.
- Open it to see what it is sending and to set how it listens to redstone: not at all, on only with a signal, or off with a signal.
- Point one at a full battery, or at nothing, and it keeps its power apart from the ready cost.

See [Emitters](../lasers/emitters.md) for the tiers side by side, and [Lenses](../lasers/lenses/overview.md) to give its laser a job.
