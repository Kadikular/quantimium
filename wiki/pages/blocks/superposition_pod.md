The Superposition Pod holds one body: yours while you stand in it, a **double** of you while you are somewhere else. From an empty pod you can swap into any of your doubles, and the body you stood in stays behind.

- Build it on a 3×3 cradle ([[block:pod_cradle]] at the corners and under the capsule, [[block:pod_plating]] or a module on each edge).
- Use it with a [[item:sophon]] to unfold your double into it. Sneak and use it with an empty hand to fold the double back into its Sophon.
- Stand in an empty pod and sneak (or use it) to open its screen and swap.
- Buffer {{c:SuperpositionPodBlockEntity.ENERGY_CAPACITY}} FE, input {{c:SuperpositionPodBlockEntity.ENERGY_MAX_RECEIVE}} FE/t. A swap in one dimension costs {{c:Superposition.FE_PER_BLOCK}} FE a block, at most {{c:Superposition.MAX_LOCAL_COST}} FE. To another dimension, {{c:Superposition.CROSS_DIMENSION_COST}} FE.
