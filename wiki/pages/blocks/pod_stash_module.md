A [[block:superposition_pod]] module, set in one of the four edges of its [[block:pod_cradle]]. It works while the pod holds one of your doubles.

Opens your stash from anywhere while the pod holds one of your doubles. The stash is a chest's worth of room kept out of phase, the same whichever pod opens it. Press the stash key (B by default) or **Stash** on a pod's screen.

Draws no power.

See the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md#modules) page.
