The Quantum Observation Chamber is the powered Observation Chamber. It collapses [[item:unrealised_matter]] on its own, and connects to pipes on every side.

- Every {{c:QuantumObservationChamberBlockEntity.CYCLE_TICKS|s}} it collapses up to {{c:QuantumObservationChamberBlockEntity.BATCH}} Matter, for {{c:QuantumObservationChamberBlockEntity.FE_PER_COLLAPSE}} FE each (more in an anomalous chunk).
- Three input slots, eight result slots, and a slot for Trace.
- Buffer {{c:QuantumObservationChamberBlockEntity.ENERGY_CAPACITY}} FE, input {{c:QuantumObservationChamberBlockEntity.ENERGY_MAX_RECEIVE}} FE/t.
- Faces are configurable, with auto in and auto out.

See the [Quantum Observation Chamber](../machines/quantum-observation-chamber.md) page.
