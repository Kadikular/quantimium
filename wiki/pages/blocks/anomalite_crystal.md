The Anomalite Crystal is a parasite that grows on powered machines in an uncontained anomalous field, draining their power. You can see and mine it only from the mirror.

- It has four stages. A grown crystal breaks into 3 [[item:anomalite_shard]]s; before that, it drops a little [[item:anomalite_dust]].
- A laser with a [[item:harvest_lens]] through a [[block:rift_lens]] can cut a grown one from the real world.

See [Anomalite](../mirror/anomalite.md) for where they grow, what they cost and how to harvest them.
