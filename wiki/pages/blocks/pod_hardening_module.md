A [[block:superposition_pod]] module, set in one of the four edges of its [[block:pod_cradle]]. It works while the pod holds one of your doubles.

Gives you Resistance I anywhere in the pod's dimension, while the pod holds one of your doubles. It gives out when you stand in Critical anomaly or worse, and doesn't stack with another.
