The Anomaly Containment Hall controller is the heart of the [Anomaly Containment Hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md). Formed with glass, plinths and one to four attunement arms, it holds a chunk's field and pulls anomaly (and only anomaly) down.

- More arms, more field: from 10,000 capacity over 3×3 chunks with one arm to 1,000,000 over 5×5 with four.
- Its cell can hold [the Veiled](../mirror/veiled.md), at the price of Rift Residue.
- Draw is {{c:ContainmentHallBlockEntity.FE_PER_TICK_BASE}} FE/t plus {{c:ContainmentHallBlockEntity.FE_PER_ARM}} per arm.

See the [Anomaly Containment Hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md) page for the numbers.
