The Horizon Core is the center of the [Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md), where its [[item:singularity]] is seated.

- Use a Singularity on the core to seat it. Sneak-use with an empty hand, or break the core, to take it out. It carries everything the Reactor holds.
- Its screen lists everything the Reactor holds and could make, like an ME terminal. Click an item for one, shift-click for a stack.
- Stores {{c:HorizonCoreBlockEntity.ENERGY_CAPACITY}} FE, taking up to {{c:HorizonCoreBlockEntity.MAX_RECEIVE}} FE/t through Energy ports.
- Capacity: {{c:HorizonCoreBlockEntity.BASE_CAPACITY}} items, four times that for each ring of Ring Emitters.
