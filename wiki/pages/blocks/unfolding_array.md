The Unfolding Array makes a double of you. It is a low pad with an [[block:array_pylon]] on each diagonal corner (a 3×3 with the edges left open so you can walk on).

- Put in a [[item:semi_stable_tesseract]] to fold you into, {{c:UnfoldingArrayBlockEntity.FRAGMENTS}} [[item:anomaly_fragment]]s and {{c:UnfoldingArrayBlockEntity.RESIDUE}} [[item:rift_residue]], stand on the pad and press **Unfold**.
- Over {{c:UnfoldingArrayBlockEntity.UNFOLD_TICKS|s}} your double peels away beside you, to be folded into a [[item:sophon]] bound to you.
- It draws {{c:UnfoldingArrayBlockEntity.FE_PER_TICK}} FE/t as it goes, from a {{c:UnfoldingArrayBlockEntity.ENERGY_CAPACITY}} FE buffer. Step off the pad and it waits for you, but loses ground while you are gone.
- You can have 4 Sophons at once, folded or in pods (`maxDoubles` in the `superposition` config). The Array refuses a fifth.
