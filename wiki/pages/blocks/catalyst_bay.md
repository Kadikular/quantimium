The Catalyst Bay is a window in the [Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md)'s floor onto a pocket of void, and holds up to {{c:CatalystBayBlockEntity.SLOTS}} catalysts: machines, crafting tables, [[item:folded_tesseract]]s.

- Use it to open a 2×2 grid, one catalyst per quarter. A Reactor takes up to eight bays, in place of plinth and anywhere but under the core.
- Each catalyst orbits the horizon as a moon, which flares when a craft uses it.
- Each bay has a filter: an output list (which of its catalysts' recipes the Reactor may use) and an input list (which items they may use up). Each list has {{c:CatalystBayBlockEntity.FILTER_SLOTS}} entries, allow or deny.
