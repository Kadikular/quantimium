Holds **flux**, and only flux, under a ceiling in every chunk of the 3×3 round it. It's the one block
that throws flux away, and it's for the places you don't want any:

- **Firebreaks.** Past its containment, a hot base's field spreads and turns the land round it
  dangerous. A Suppressor at the edge stops the spread before it reaches your house, a village or a
  farm.
- **Starving rifts.** Flux sets how far a [rift](../mechanics/flux-rifts.md) can grow, and the Veiled
  only comes at Critical flux. Keep flux down near a rift and it stays small.
- **Quiet zones.** A mirror entrance or a storeroom you'd like calm.

[[mechanic:field.suppressor]] Set its ceiling with the **C** tab: Low, Medium, High or Clear. It holds
flux just under the top of that band (80, 800 or 8,000), or at nothing for Clear, taking only what
each chunk has over its mark, in the field's own once-a-second step, so it lands on the mark and
stays. It pulls up to 1,500 flux a second, a fifth of it from its own chunk, and draws up to 40 FE/t
in proportion to what it takes. It makes no fragments: those come from chewing anomaly (see the
[[block:basic_anomaly_siphon]]).
