Gray grass that grows in the mirror.

Mirror flora is only a record. Players outside the mirror, observers and other mods see air. It grows denser as the field gets hotter, and within a few chunks of someone in the mirror it grows and withers every second. Elsewhere, with no one to see it, every half minute in the background. You can't get it as an item. See [the mirror phase](../mirror/mirror-phase.md).
