The Anomaly Siphon keeps anomaly in the 3×3 chunks around it under a ceiling you choose, and yields [[item:anomaly_fragment]]s.

- Set the ceiling with the **ceiling** tab: **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Clear**. It holds anomaly just under the top of the band (80, 800, 8,000), or at nothing for Clear.
- It pulls anomaly only, so your flux stays. Up to {{c:FieldControlBlockEntity.PULL_PER_SECOND}} a second.
- Draw: up to {{c:FieldControlBlockEntity.PULL_FE_PER_TICK}} FE/t, in proportion to what it takes.
- One fragment per {{c:FieldControlBlockEntity.ANOMALY_PER_FRAGMENT}} anomaly pulled, into a slot pipes and hoppers can take from.

Made from a [[block:basic_anomaly_siphon]] in four Anomaly Fragments, on polished deepslate with a block of redstone. See [Field control](../field-control/overview.md#holding-anomaly-down).
