# Unrealized Ore

<ItemImage id="quantimium:unrealised_ore" scale="2" />

Unrealized Ore looks like an ordinary ore of your pack until you mine it, then drops <ItemLink id="quantimium:unrealised_matter" />.

- **Where:** underground in stone and deepslate, between about y -40 and 56, in veins of up to 7, six attempts per chunk. Packs can change what it looks like and where it generates (see [For pack makers](../guides/pack-makers.md)).
- **Drops:** Unrealized Matter. Fortune gives more.
- **Silk Touch** keeps the ore block. Every machine that takes Matter takes the block as well: one block is one Matter with no history yet.

Take the Matter to an <ItemLink id="quantimium:observation_chamber" /> or a <ItemLink id="quantimium:materialiser" />.
