# Horizon Core

<ItemImage id="quantimium:horizon_core" scale="2" />

Part of the [Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md).

The Horizon Core is the center of the [Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md), where its <ItemLink id="quantimium:singularity" /> is seated.

- Use a Singularity on the core to seat it. Sneak-use with an empty hand, or break the core, to take it out. It carries everything the Reactor holds.
- Its screen lists everything the Reactor holds and could make, like an ME terminal. Click an item for one, shift-click for a stack.
- Stores 20,000,000 FE, taking up to 500,000 FE/t through Energy ports.
- Capacity: 1,000,000 items, four times that for each ring of Ring Emitters.
