# Anomalite Dust

<ItemImage id="quantimium:anomalite_dust" scale="2" />

Anomalite Dust is what a crystal broken before it is grown leaves, and what a crushed shard becomes.

- The <ItemLink id="quantimium:quantum_foundry_controller" /> refines it back into a <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomalite_shard" />.
- The <ItemLink id="quantimium:materialiser" /> is paid in it. A <ItemLink id="quantimium:crushing_lens" /> can turn shards into dust for that.
