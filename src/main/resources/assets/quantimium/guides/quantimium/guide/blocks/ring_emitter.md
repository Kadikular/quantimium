# Ring Emitter

<ItemImage id="quantimium:ring_emitter" scale="2" />

Part of the [Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md).

Ring Emitters drive the rings that hold the [Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md)'s horizon open.

- Place them on the plinth around the core in facing pairs, four blocks out: east and west, north and south, or diagonally three out each way.
- Each whole pair drives one ring. Each ring quadruples what the horizon holds: 1,000,000 items with one ring, four million with two, sixteen million with three.
- Each emitter draws 1,000 FE/t. Unpowered, the rings go down and the Reactor takes nothing in and makes nothing, but keeps everything.
