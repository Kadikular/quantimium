# Fold Rail

<ItemImage id="quantimium:fold_rail" scale="2" />

Part of the [Fold Chamber](../multiblocks/fold-chamber.md).

The Fold Rail is an edge of a [Fold Chamber](../multiblocks/fold-chamber.md)'s frame, between two <ItemLink id="quantimium:fold_pylon" />s. A rail turns to its edge as you place it, and rails in a row read as one tube. The twelve edges are rails, except the middle of a bottom edge, which is the <ItemLink id="quantimium:fold_core" />.
