# Quantum Foundry Plinth

<ItemImage id="quantimium:quantum_foundry_plinth" scale="2" />

Part of the [Quantum Foundry](../multiblocks/quantum-foundry.md).

The Quantum Foundry Plinth is the floor of the [Quantum Foundry](../multiblocks/quantum-foundry.md), and the base and cap of the [Anomaly Containment Hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md).

- A 3×3 ring of plinth goes around the controller.
- The Foundry takes power through any plinth block.
