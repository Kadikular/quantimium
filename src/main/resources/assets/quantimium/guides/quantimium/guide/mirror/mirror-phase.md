# The mirror phase

*The world, with the volume turned down.*

The mirror is a gray, quiet copy of the world, laid over it. Players who **phase** into it still walk on the same terrain, but they cannot touch the real world: they cannot break or place blocks, use things, or hurt anything real. What lives there can hurt them.

A few things still work from the mirror: **doors, trapdoors, fence gates, buttons and levers** (`#quantimium:mirror_usable`). They work on the real world, so a door you open from the mirror stands open for everyone, and a lever drives real redstone.

## Getting in and out

- **Tears.** Uncontained chunks at Medium anomaly or higher tear open on their own for about a minute: the hotter the field, the more often. Walk through one to phase. A way back opens 10–20 blocks away and lasts 30 seconds.
- **The Stabilized Portal.** A 4×5 frame of <ItemLink id="quantimium:stabilised_portal_frame" /> with a 2×3 inside, lit with a <ItemLink id="quantimium:rift_seed" />: a wound opened on purpose and held in the frame. It works from both sides.
- **Leaving.** Walk back through a tear or portal, die, or wait: a five-minute failsafe always brings you home. A collapsing rift throws everyone near it out.

## What is there

- **Flora.** Gray plants on every surface, denser as the field gets hotter. They are only records: observers and other mods see air. Within a few chunks of someone in the mirror they grow and wither every second. Elsewhere, with no one there to see, every half minute, in the background. The plants are <ItemLink id="quantimium:mirror_short_grass" />, <ItemLink id="quantimium:mirror_tall_grass" />, <ItemLink id="quantimium:mirror_vine" />, <ItemLink id="quantimium:mirror_dead_bush" /> and <ItemLink id="quantimium:mirror_hanging_roots" />.
- **Mirror mites.** They roll in around phased players from Medium flux or anomaly, but only hunt from Medium anomaly up. They grow stronger at High, Critical and Singularity. One killed by a player leaves a <ItemLink id="quantimium:quantimium_trace" /> a quarter of the time, in the mirror, for whoever killed it.
- **Anomalite crystals.** They grow on real machines but can only be seen and mined from the mirror, or cut from the real world by a laser with a Harvest Lens sent through a <ItemLink id="quantimium:rift_lens" />. See [Anomalite](anomalite.md).
- **Souls.** Living things on the other side show as pale folding tesseracts.
- **Rifts**, and [the Veiled](veiled.md).
