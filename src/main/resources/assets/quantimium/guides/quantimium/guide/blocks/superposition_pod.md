# Superposition Pod

<ItemImage id="quantimium:superposition_pod" scale="2" />

Part of the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md).

The Superposition Pod holds one body: yours while you stand in it, a **double** of you while you are somewhere else. From an empty pod you can swap into any of your doubles, and the body you stood in stays behind.

- Build it on a 3×3 cradle (<ItemLink id="quantimium:pod_cradle" /> at the corners and under the capsule, <ItemLink id="quantimium:pod_plating" /> or a module on each edge).
- Use it with a <ItemLink id="quantimium:sophon" /> to unfold your double into it. Sneak and use it with an empty hand to fold the double back into its Sophon.
- Stand in an empty pod and sneak (or use it) to open its screen and swap.
- Buffer 2,000,000 FE, input 1,000 FE/t. A swap in one dimension costs 100 FE a block, at most 500,000 FE. To another dimension, 1,000,000 FE.

See the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md) page.
