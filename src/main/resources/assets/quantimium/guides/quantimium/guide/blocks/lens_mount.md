# Lens Mount

<ItemImage id="quantimium:lens_mount" scale="2" />

The Lens Mount holds four lenses in a row. A laser goes straight through it, in by its back and out of its front, through its lenses from left to right.

- A laser from any other side, or at an angle, stops on it.
- Open it with an empty hand. Sneak-click or wrench a side to point it there.
- Down each side, four slot panels show what is fitted: dark when empty, the lens's color when there is one. A button under each lens opens its settings, lit when it has any.
- A lensed laser is tinted by what it does. It still carries power to whatever it ends on.

See [Lenses](../lasers/lenses/overview.md), [action lenses](../lasers/lenses/actions.md) and [modifier lenses](../lasers/lenses/modifiers.md).
