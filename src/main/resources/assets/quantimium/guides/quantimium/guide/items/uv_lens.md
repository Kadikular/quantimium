# UV Lens

<ItemImage id="quantimium:uv_lens" scale="2" />

UV Lens hides the laser from anyone who is not holding a wrench or the Laser Linker.

## How it works

- It stays on the laser from the lens onward.
- Wrenches and the Laser Linker still show it, so you can build and line things up.

## Energy

Free.
