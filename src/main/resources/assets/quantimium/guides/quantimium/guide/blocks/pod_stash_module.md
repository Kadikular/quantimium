# Pod Stash Module

<ItemImage id="quantimium:pod_stash_module" scale="2" />

Part of the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md).

A <ItemLink id="quantimium:superposition_pod" /> module, set in one of the four edges of its <ItemLink id="quantimium:pod_cradle" />. It works while the pod holds one of your doubles.

Opens your stash from anywhere while the pod holds one of your doubles. The stash is a chest's worth of room kept out of phase, the same whichever pod opens it. Press the stash key (B by default) or **Stash** on a pod's screen.

Draws no power.

See the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md) page.
