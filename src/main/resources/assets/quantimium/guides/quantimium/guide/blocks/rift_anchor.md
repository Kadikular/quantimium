# Rift Anchor

<ItemImage id="quantimium:rift_anchor" scale="2" />

Part of the [Stabilised rift](../multiblocks/stabilised-rift.md).

Footing for a stabilised rift: seeded with a Rift Seed or slipped under a natural rift. Claims up to six Rift Stabilisers 2–6 blocks out; with 3+ powered and in sight the rift is held, grows 10 min a stage, loses its hazards bar a short-range shock, and makes residue (stage × stabilisers). Array fails, it is a wild rift again.

The footing of a [stabilised rift](../multiblocks/stabilised-rift.md). Use a <ItemLink id="quantimium:rift_seed" /> on it,
or place it under a natural rift, then surround it with <ItemLink id="quantimium:rift_stabiliser" />s.
