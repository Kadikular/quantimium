# Logistics Receptacle

<ItemImage id="quantimium:logistics_receptacle" scale="2" />

The Logistics Receptacle is a Laser Focus attachment. It puts the inventory behind it on a logistics network.

- Goes on the face against the inventory (a chest, a machine, a drawer), and works while a laser reaches the focus.
- Roles: **storage**, **provider** (passive or active) and **requester** (passive or active), with a priority and a nine-entry filter. **Keep stocked** keeps a count in the inventory.
- On a machine that takes power, it can also power the machine from the controller's laser.
- Moves up to 16 items a second.

There is also a standalone Logistics Receptacle block. See [Logistics](../lasers/logistics.md).
