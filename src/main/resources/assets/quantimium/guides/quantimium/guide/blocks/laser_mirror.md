# Laser Mirror

<ItemImage id="quantimium:laser_mirror" scale="2" />

The Laser Mirror reflects a laser, as a real mirror would. It is a silver plate on a steel stand.

- A laser bounces and goes on as the same laser, with its own actions, so lasers crossing one mirror never mix.
- Click a face to set the plate: the middle is flat (the laser goes straight back), the inner ring turns it 22.5°, the outer ring 45° (a right angle). Sneak-click to move the stand.
- A laser on the back or edge of the plate stops. A redstone signal turns the mirror off.
- Each bounce takes 0.25% of what the laser carries, as flux. Each run after a bounce reaches as far as one from the emitter.

See [Mirrors and splitters](../lasers/mirrors.md) for angles, and [Optical cavities](../lasers/cavities.md) for two mirrors facing each other.
