# Anomalite Crystal

<ItemImage id="quantimium:anomalite_crystal" scale="2" />

The Anomalite Crystal is a parasite that grows on powered machines in an uncontained anomalous field, draining their power. You can see and mine it only from the mirror.

- It has four stages. A grown crystal breaks into 3 <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomalite_shard" />s; before that, it drops a little <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomalite_dust" />.
- A laser with a <ItemLink id="quantimium:harvest_lens" /> through a <ItemLink id="quantimium:rift_lens" /> can cut a grown one from the real world.

See [Anomalite](../mirror/anomalite.md) for where they grow, what they cost and how to harvest them.
