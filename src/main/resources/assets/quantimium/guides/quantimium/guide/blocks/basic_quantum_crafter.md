# Basic Quantum Crafter

<ItemImage id="quantimium:basic_quantum_crafter" scale="2" />

Early vanilla crafts (table / furnace / stonecutter); normal FE costs plus a separate 32-run coherence reserve regenerated linearly over 100 ticks. All-face hopper I/O, no auto-push or side config.

The low-tier [Quantum Crafter](../machines/quantum-crafter.md): vanilla workstations only, a
1,000,000 FE buffer, and a coherence reserve of 32 runs
that refills every 5 seconds. See [The Basic Quantum Crafter](../machines/quantum-crafter.md).
