# Entity Filter Lens

<ItemImage id="quantimium:entity_filter_lens" scale="2" />

Entity Filter Lens limits Damage, Healing, Freezing and Interact lasers to the living things it lets through.

## How it works

- Nine entries take spawn eggs, set as allow or deny, for kinds of mob.
- Sneak-use the lens on a creature or player to list **just that one** (shown as their head or spawn egg, named). Sneak-use it in the air to list yourself. Click an entry out of its slot to take it off the list.
- Toggles to light up hostile, animals or players (none lit means any).
- **Spare the young**, and a **spare** count (shift for tens): it hits only those beyond that many, so a pen keeps its breeding pair.

## Energy

Free.

## Examples

- **Personal healing aura.** Healing with a lens listing you heals only you.
- **Pen culling.** Damage with spare set to 2 leaves a breeding pair.
