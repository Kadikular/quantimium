# Quantum Observation Chamber

<ItemImage id="quantimium:quantum_observation_chamber" scale="2" />

The Quantum Observation Chamber is the powered Observation Chamber. It collapses <ItemLink id="quantimium:unrealised_matter" /> on its own, and connects to pipes on every side.

- Every 0.5 s it collapses up to 4 Matter, for 400 FE each (more in an anomalous chunk).
- Three input slots, eight result slots, and a slot for Trace.
- Buffer 200,000 FE, input 4,000 FE/t.
- Faces are configurable, with auto in and auto out.

See the [Quantum Observation Chamber](../machines/quantum-observation-chamber.md) page.
