# Flux Maintainer

<ItemImage id="quantimium:flux_maintainer" scale="2" />

The Flux Maintainer keeps a chunk's flux at or above a floor you choose: Medium, High or Critical.

- Set the floor with the **floor** tab on the left.
- It raises flux with pure flux, so no anomaly comes with it. Flat out it puts about as much flux into its chunk as 100,000 FE/t of ordinary work.
- Draw: 10,000 FE/t while raising. Buffer 2,000,000 FE.
- It says **Raising**, **Holding** or **No power**.

Made from a <ItemLink id="quantimium:quantum_exciter" /> in four glowstone, on polished deepslate with a block of redstone. See [Field control](../field-control/overview.md).
