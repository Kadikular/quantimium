# Anomaly Siphon

<ItemImage id="quantimium:anomaly_siphon" scale="2" />

The Anomaly Siphon keeps anomaly in the 3×3 chunks around it under a ceiling you choose, and yields <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomaly_fragment" />s.

- Set the ceiling with the **ceiling** tab: **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Clear**. It holds anomaly just under the top of the band (80, 800, 8,000), or at nothing for Clear.
- It pulls anomaly only, so your flux stays. Up to 6,000 a second.
- Draw: up to 160 FE/t, in proportion to what it takes.
- One fragment per 24,000 anomaly pulled, into a slot pipes and hoppers can take from.

Made from a <ItemLink id="quantimium:basic_anomaly_siphon" /> in four Anomaly Fragments, on polished deepslate with a block of redstone. See [Field control](../field-control/overview.md).
