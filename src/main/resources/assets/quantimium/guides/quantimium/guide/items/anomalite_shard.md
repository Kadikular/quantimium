# Anomalite Shard

<ItemImage id="quantimium:anomalite_shard" scale="2" />

An Anomalite Shard is what a fully grown <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomalite_crystal" /> breaks into: 3 shards. See [Anomalite](../mirror/anomalite.md) for how to harvest a crystal.

Shards also come from <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomalite_dust" /> (refined in the Foundry) and from an <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomalite_lattice" />. They make lattices, cells and emitters.
