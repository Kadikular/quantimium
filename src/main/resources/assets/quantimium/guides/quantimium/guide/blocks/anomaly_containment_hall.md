# Anomaly Containment Hall

<ItemImage id="quantimium:anomaly_containment_hall" scale="2" />

Part of the [Anomaly Containment Hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md).

Late 5-tall cell: 3×3 plinth base and cap, three layers of hollow Quantum Attuned Glass, 1–4 reused Foundry arms. `120 + 60×arms` FE/t; capacity holding all of High / Critical / Critical / Singularity over the 3×3 / 3×3 / 5×5 / 5×5; pulls 600 anomaly a second per arm but never touches flux. Holds the Veiled; burns 1 Rift Residue per 5 minutes while holding, fed by hand or through any arm (16 stored).

The controller of the [Anomaly Containment Hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md).
