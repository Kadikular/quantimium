# Pod Logistics Module

<ItemImage id="quantimium:pod_logistics_module" scale="2" />

A <ItemLink id="quantimium:superposition_pod" /> module, set in one of the four edges of its <ItemLink id="quantimium:pod_cradle" />. It works while the pod holds one of your doubles.

A <ItemLink id="quantimium:logistics_terminal" /> in the pod's cradle. Point a Logistics Controller's laser at it, and the logistics key (V by default) opens that network's terminal from anywhere while the pod holds one of your doubles.

Draw: as the controller pays.

See the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md) page.
