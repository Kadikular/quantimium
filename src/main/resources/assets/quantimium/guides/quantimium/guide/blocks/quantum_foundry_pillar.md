# Attunement Pillar

<ItemImage id="quantimium:quantum_foundry_pillar" scale="2" />

Part of the [Quantum Foundry](../multiblocks/quantum-foundry.md).

The Attunement Pillar draws on the local field for the [Foundry](../multiblocks/quantum-foundry.md). One stands at the end of each arm, after the conduit.

- Each recipe ingredient sits on its own pillar, so a recipe with more ingredients needs more arms.
- An <ItemLink id="quantimium:quantum_foundry_attunement_tank" /> goes on top.
