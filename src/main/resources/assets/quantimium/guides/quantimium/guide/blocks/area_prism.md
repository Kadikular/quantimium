# Area Prism

<ItemImage id="quantimium:area_prism" scale="2" />

The Area Prism spreads the actions of the laser reaching it over an area of any size. Harvest cuts each block there in turn, Damage hurts and Pickup gathers whatever is in it, and Placement fills each free space resting on a block.

- Set the area with the <ItemLink id="quantimium:laser_linker" /> (two corners, then the prism), or in the prism's screen as a cube or sphere with a radius.
- It works only on what its laser reaches (8 blocks with an action, more with Range Lenses) and can see along a clear line.
- Work in an area costs 1.5 times as much for a cut or a placement.
- Order: nearest first, top down (a quarry) or bottom up.

See [Prisms](../lasers/prisms.md) and [Lenses](../lasers/lenses/overview.md).
