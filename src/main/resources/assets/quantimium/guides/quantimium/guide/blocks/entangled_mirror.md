# Entangled Mirror

<ItemImage id="quantimium:entangled_mirror" scale="2" />

The Entangled Mirror sends on the laser of another laser block, any distance away, through anything, in the same dimension.

- Made in pairs in the <ItemLink id="quantimium:quantum_foundry_controller" />, from a <ItemLink id="quantimium:tesseract" /> and two Laser Mirrors.
- Bind a Tesseract to the laser block the laser should come from (sneak and use it on that block), then use it on the mirror. It floats in the mirror's window.
- The jump takes 5% of what crosses, as flux. Several mirrors can draw on one block.
- Both blocks must be loaded. A Tesseract bound to another dimension sends nothing.
- A redstone signal switches it off.

See [Mirrors and splitters](../lasers/mirrors.md).
