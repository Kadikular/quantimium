# Laser Tank

<ItemImage id="quantimium:laser_tank" scale="2" />

The Laser Tank is a laser's fluid end, as the <ItemLink id="quantimium:laser_port" /> is its item end. It passes a laser through (in by its back, out of its face) and holds 4 tanks of 16,000 mB, one fluid in each.

- **Collect** (the default): catches what an Extract Lens drains, for pipes to take away.
- **Send**: sends what it holds down the laser, to an Insert Lens or another Laser Tank further along.
- **Both**: catches, then sends down.
- Use a bucket on it, or click a tank in its screen with one, to fill or empty it. Pipes drain it, and fill it only while it sends.
- Use the Sides tab for pipes, and for faces that push to or pull from what is beside them.
- Set a filter on what it catches: nine fluid entries, as a whitelist or a blacklist. Click an entry with a bucket, or drag a fluid in from JEI.

See [Laser Ports](../lasers/ports.md).
