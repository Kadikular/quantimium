# Reactor Logistics Port

<ItemImage id="quantimium:reactor_logistics_port" scale="2" />

The Reactor Logistics Port joins the Quantimium Reactor to a logistics network both ways. Put it on the Reactor's plinth rim, where a controller's laser ends.

- To the network it is storage, at its priority. It offers what the Reactor holds, and what could be made from it.
- Set to share (the default), the network's contents are the Reactor's to make with.
- A laser ending on it also powers the Reactor.

See [Logistics](../lasers/logistics.md) and the [Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md).
