# Budding Anomalite

<ItemImage id="quantimium:budding_anomalite" scale="2" />

Budding Anomalite grows <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomalite_crystal" />s in the mirror while it is powered. It ignores flux, anomaly and containment, so you can farm in a quiet chunk. It pays a flat FE/t, and its crystals never drain another machine, shrink, or count against the chunk's crystal cap.

Craft it from gold ingots, a lattice, shards and a block of amethyst. See [Anomalite](../mirror/anomalite.md).
