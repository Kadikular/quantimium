# Materializer

<ItemImage id="quantimium:materialiser" scale="2" />

The Materializer turns <ItemLink id="quantimium:unrealised_matter" /> into the ore, in the form, that you choose. Every form of every ore the Matter could become is on its screen, searchable as JEI is.

- Click a form for a stack of it, right-click for half, shift-click into your inventory. Middle-click a form to **export** it: it is what pipes and hoppers see and take, made as they take it.
- Each Matter costs **4,000 FE**, flux from its own chunk (25 × the ore's value) and **Anomalite Dust** (1.2 × the ore's value over the average roll in that band).
- The flux band caps the choice: nothing at Low, commons at Medium, uncommons at High, rares from Critical. Very rare ores are never choosable.
- It gives what one ore gives on average, rounded down: three raw copper a Matter, every time.
- A bound <ItemLink id="quantimium:tesseract" /> in the Matter or dust slot reads the inventory it links to.
- Buffer 200,000 FE, input 4,000 FE/t.

See [Unrealized Matter](../items/unrealised_matter.md) for the details.
