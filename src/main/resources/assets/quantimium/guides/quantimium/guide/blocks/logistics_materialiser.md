# Logistics Materializer

<ItemImage id="quantimium:logistics_materialiser" scale="2" />

The Logistics Materializer offers a whole logistics network to any mod. Place it where a controller's laser ends.

- An AE2 storage bus or a pipe on any face sees everything storage holds and providers offer, takes from it, and puts things away into storage.
- Moves happen as part of the other side's transaction, so one called off puts everything back.
- Click it to choose what it offers: stored and craftable (the default), stored, or craftable.

See [Logistics](../lasers/logistics.md).
