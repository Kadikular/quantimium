# Rift Seed

<ItemImage id="quantimium:rift_seed" scale="2" />

> *In game:* A wound waiting to open. Plant it where anomaly runs Medium or higher, or on a Rift Anchor. Lights a Stabilised Portal.

A wound waiting to open, and the way to a rift when the world won't give you one.

- **On the ground**, it opens a wild stage 1 [flux rift](../mechanics/flux-rifts.md), but only
  in an uncontained chunk at Medium anomaly or higher. Anywhere else it fizzles and you keep it.
  Leave the rift to grow before you close it: a stage 1 rift pays no <ItemLink id="quantimium:rift_residue" />.
- **On a <ItemLink id="quantimium:rift_anchor" />**, it opens a held rift, in any field: see the
  [stabilised rift](../multiblocks/stabilised-rift.md).
- **In a <ItemLink id="quantimium:stabilised_portal_frame" />**, it lights the portal to the
  [mirror](../concepts/mirror-phase.md).

It's a Mid-tier craft (Fragments, Anomalite and an eye of ender), so you can reach High, which needs
Residue, without waiting on a tear to fail.
