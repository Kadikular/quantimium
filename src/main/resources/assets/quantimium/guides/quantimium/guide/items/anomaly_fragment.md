# Anomaly Fragment

<ItemImage id="quantimium:anomaly_fragment" scale="2" />

> *In game:* Produced by Anomaly Siphons.

Anomaly Fragments are produced by the Anomaly Siphons as they hold anomaly down: the <ItemLink id="quantimium:basic_anomaly_siphon" />, the <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomaly_siphon" /> and the <ItemLink id="quantimium:field_regulator" />.

- One fragment per 24,000 anomaly pulled, into a slot pipes and hoppers can take from. A Siphon in a Quantum Simulator makes them a batch at a time.
- A common ingredient in Mid-tier machines, and in the Superposition Pod's modules.
