# Tether

<ItemImage id="quantimium:tether" scale="2" />

The Tether is a Superposition Pod's screen in your hand. Use it anywhere to see your doubles and swap into one, across dimensions too.

- It pays from its own 1,000,000 FE buffer. A Charge module keeps it topped up.
- **The catch:** the body you leave stands where you were, a **field double**, frozen in place with no pod and nothing guarding it. Hostile mobs within 12 blocks come for it, and other players can hit it when PvP is on. With its 20 health gone it is knocked out into its Sophon.
- Your field double is on every pod's screen as *In the field*, so you can tether home and swap back to where you left off from a pod. Sneak-use your own field double to fold it back into its Sophon.
- The Tether only reaches doubles in pods, never one body in the open to another.
