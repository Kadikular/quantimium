# Singularity

<ItemImage id="quantimium:singularity" scale="2" />

> *In game:* It's safe, I promise.

A Singularity is a Tesseract collapsed into a black hole. It is the horizon of a [Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md), and holds everything the Reactor holds.

## How to get it

Put an **unbound** <ItemLink id="quantimium:tesseract" /> in the core of an **empty** [Fold Chamber](../multiblocks/fold-chamber.md) at least 9 blocks every way and press Fold. With nothing inside to hold the volume open it collapses, for 1,000,000 FE.

## Using it

- Seat it in a <ItemLink id="quantimium:horizon_core" /> by using it on the core. It carries everything with it when you take it out.
- A dropped Singularity never despawns and doesn't burn, in fire or lava. Only the void takes it.
- Carrying one that holds something earns *Should you be holding that?*
- Its tooltip says it is safe.
