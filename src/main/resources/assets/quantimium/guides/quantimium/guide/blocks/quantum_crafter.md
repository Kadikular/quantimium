# Quantum Crafter

<ItemImage id="quantimium:quantum_crafter" scale="2" />

Instant electric / mod crafts from catalyst + inputs / Links; FE + flux emit. Catalyst tiers need a flux band; the tax falls in a hotter field.

Makes another machine's recipes instantly, for a price in FE. The full write-up (catalysts, costs,
batches, automation and Tesseract chains) is on the [Quantum Crafter](../machines/quantum-crafter.md)
page.
