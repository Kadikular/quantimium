# Veil Thread

<ItemImage id="quantimium:veil_thread" scale="2" />

> *In game:* Drawn from a Veiled held in a Containment Hall.

A strand of the Veiled, and the only way to get one is to draw it from a Veiled held in an [Anomaly Containment Hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md).

## How to get it

1. **Hold a Veiled.** Wear one down and let a powered, fed Containment Hall take it (see [Ending a fight](../mirror/veiled.md)).
2. **Aim a laser at the cell.** Point a laser through the middle of any wall of the hall's glass. A <ItemLink id="quantimium:rift_lens" /> against the glass seats onto it with a studded collar, so the laser reaches the cell from the mirror's side.
3. **Add a Pickup Lens.** The laser needs a <ItemLink id="quantimium:pickup_lens" /> on it. It is only the mirror's Pickup that draws thread.
4. **Wait.** It draws one thread every 3 min at 120 FE/t. One draw to a hall at a time. The thread goes up the laser to a collecting <ItemLink id="quantimium:laser_port" />.

See [Networks and rules](../lasers/networks.md) for how a laser behaves in the mirror.

## Uses

Veil Thread goes into the top laser parts: the <ItemLink id="quantimium:laser_emitter_3" /> and the <ItemLink id="quantimium:ring_emitter" />.
