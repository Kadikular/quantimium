# Laser Port

<ItemImage id="quantimium:laser_port" scale="2" />

The Laser Port is a laser's item end. It passes a laser through (in by its back, out of its face) and holds nine slots.

- **Collect** (the default): catches what a Pickup Lens brings back, for pipes and hoppers.
- **Send**: sends what is in its slots down the laser, to a Placement, Insert or Interact Lens further along.
- **Both**: what comes back can go straight back down. A port of seeds on a wheat field catches the harvest and sows the field again.
- Set a filter on what it catches. Use the Sides tab for pipes and hoppers.

See [Laser Ports](../lasers/ports.md).
