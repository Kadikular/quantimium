# Laser Prism

<ItemImage id="quantimium:laser_prism" scale="2" />

The Laser Prism sends the laser that reaches it to every block it is linked to, in a straight line at any angle, sharing it by what each asks for. It also joins lasers from several emitters into one network.

- Link it with the <ItemLink id="quantimium:laser_linker" />: use the Linker on the prism, then on each block. Up to 8 blocks, within 24 blocks.
- The laser goes in by the face you linked: the side of a machine it charges, or a Laser Port's or Lens Mount's back.
- Lasers can reach it from any side. It refocuses and taxes the laser as a relay does.
- Use an empty hand on a prism to make it keep its links aimed at the spot.

See [Prisms](../lasers/prisms.md).
