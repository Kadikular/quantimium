# Unfolding Array

<ItemImage id="quantimium:unfolding_array" scale="2" />

Part of the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md).

The Unfolding Array makes a double of you. It is a low pad with an <ItemLink id="quantimium:array_pylon" /> on each diagonal corner (a 3×3 with the edges left open so you can walk on).

- Put in a <ItemLink id="quantimium:semi_stable_tesseract" /> to fold you into, 4 <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomaly_fragment" />s and 1 <ItemLink id="quantimium:rift_residue" />, stand on the pad and press **Unfold**.
- Over 30 s your double peels away beside you, to be folded into a <ItemLink id="quantimium:sophon" /> bound to you.
- It draws 5,000 FE/t as it goes, from a 4,000,000 FE buffer. Step off the pad and it waits for you, but loses ground while you are gone.
- You can have 4 Sophons at once, folded or in pods (`maxDoubles` in the `superposition` config). The Array refuses a fifth.
