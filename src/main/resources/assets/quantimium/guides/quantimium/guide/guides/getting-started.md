# Getting started

The first hour of Quantimium, from the first strange ore to your first crafter.

For the whole climb, chapter by chapter, and what to do when you're stuck, see [Progression](progression.md).

## 1. Unrealized Ore

<ItemLink id="quantimium:unrealised_ore" /> looks like ordinary stone or deepslate ore until you mine it. It drops <ItemLink id="quantimium:unrealised_matter" />. Fortune gives more. Silk Touch keeps the ore itself, which machines take as Matter too.

With [GuideME](https://modrinth.com/mod/guideme) installed, a Book and one Unrealized Matter make the **Quantimium Field Guide**: this wiki, in the game.

## 2. Observing it

An <ItemLink id="quantimium:observation_chamber" /> collapses Unrealized Matter into ordinary items on a redstone pulse. Now and then it leaves a <ItemLink id="quantimium:quantimium_trace" />, the mod's first real material. Later, mites killed in the [mirror](../mirror/mirror-phase.md) leave Traces too.

## 3. The first machines

- **<ItemLink id="quantimium:flux_meter" />:** reads the flux and anomaly of the chunk you stand in. Make one early.
- **<ItemLink id="quantimium:basic_decoherence_matrix" />:** an ender eye held in four Traces. It is the core of the **<ItemLink id="quantimium:basic_quantum_crafter" />**, which runs table, furnace and stonecutter recipes.
- **<ItemLink id="quantimium:flux_detector" />:** turns your chunk's band into a redstone signal.

## 4. Power and the field

From here machines run on FE. Every craft adds [flux](../concepts/flux-and-anomaly.md) to the chunks around it, and anomaly follows. Four blocks keep it in check:

- **<ItemLink id="quantimium:quantum_exciter" />:** raises flux when you need a higher band.
- **<ItemLink id="quantimium:basic_anomaly_siphon" />:** keeps anomaly down around a small workshop, and turns what it pulls into <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomaly_fragment" />s.
- **<ItemLink id="quantimium:flux_suppressor" />:** holds flux down where you want none: a firebreak around a hot base, or near a rift.
- **[Field control](../field-control/overview.md):** the stronger versions, for later.

## 5. What comes next

[Progression](progression.md) takes it from here, chapter by chapter: what each unlocks, and where players get stuck.

- The <ItemLink id="quantimium:quantum_crafter" /> and <ItemLink id="quantimium:quantum_simulator" /> take over your crafting.
- [Anomalite](../mirror/anomalite.md) crystals start growing on your machines once anomaly runs Medium (a few; more at High). They are a hazard and a resource.
- Then come the [Quantum Foundry](../multiblocks/quantum-foundry.md), the [mirror](../mirror/mirror-phase.md), [rifts](../mirror/flux-rifts.md) and [the Veiled](../mirror/veiled.md).
- [Lasers](../lasers/overview.md) carry power across a base and, with lenses, mine, fight and move items.
- At the end of it all stands the [Quantimium Reactor](../multiblocks/reactor.md).
