# Unrealizing Lens

<ItemImage id="quantimium:unrealising_lens" scale="2" />

Unrealizing Lens makes ore a Harvest laser cuts (anything in `#c:ores`) come away as <ItemLink id="quantimium:unrealised_matter" />, one per block, with no history yet.

## How it works

- A pack can turn it off in the config, where Matter would duplicate ores.

## Energy

Free.
