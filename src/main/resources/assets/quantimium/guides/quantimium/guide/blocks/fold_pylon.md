# Fold Pylon

<ItemImage id="quantimium:fold_pylon" scale="2" />

Part of the [Fold Chamber](../multiblocks/fold-chamber.md).

The Fold Pylon marks a corner of a [Fold Chamber](../multiblocks/fold-chamber.md)'s frame. A chamber needs one on each of its eight corners.
