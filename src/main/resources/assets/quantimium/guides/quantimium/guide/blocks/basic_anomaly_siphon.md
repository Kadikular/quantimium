# Basic Anomaly Siphon

<ItemImage id="quantimium:basic_anomaly_siphon" scale="2" />

The Basic Anomaly Siphon is the first answer to anomaly, and the first source of <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomaly_fragment" />s. It holds anomaly in every chunk of the 3×3 around it at the top of Low (80), pulling anomaly and only anomaly, so the flux you made stays.

- It is the early, fixed form of the [Anomaly Siphon](../field-control/overview.md): no setting to change, a twelfth of a Siphon's pull and a quarter of its power.
- It holds a small workshop, about 1,000 to 5,000 FE/t of work, at the top of Low. Much more and it is overwhelmed: a 10,000 FE/t workshop takes anomaly past it into Medium. That is the point to build a [Siphon](../field-control/overview.md) or, later, a [hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md).
- What it pulls becomes Anomaly Fragments, one per 24,000, in a slot pipes and hoppers can take from.
- It draws up to 40 FE/t, in proportion to what it takes. With nothing to take, it draws nothing.
- Its pull lands in the field's own once-a-second step, so it holds its mark steadily.
