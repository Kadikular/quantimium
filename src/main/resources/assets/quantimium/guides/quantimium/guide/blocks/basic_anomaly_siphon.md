# Basic Anomaly Siphon

<ItemImage id="quantimium:basic_anomaly_siphon" scale="2" />

The first answer to anomaly, and the first source of <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomaly_fragment" />s. It holds anomaly in
every chunk of the 3×3 round it at the top of Low (80), pulling anomaly and only anomaly, so the flux
you made stays. It's the early, fixed form of the [Anomaly Siphon](../machines/field-control.md):
no setting to change, a twelfth of a Siphon's pull and a quarter of its power.

- It holds a small workshop, about 1,000 to 5,000 FE/t of work, at
  the top of Low. Much more and it's overwhelmed: a 10,000 FE/t workshop takes anomaly past it into
  Medium. That's the point to build a [Siphon](../machines/field-control.md) or, later, a
  [hall](../multiblocks/containment-hall.md).
- What it pulls becomes Anomaly Fragments, one per 24,000, in a slot pipes and hoppers can take from.
- It draws up to 40 FE/t, in proportion to what it takes; with nothing to take, it draws nothing.
- Its pull lands in the field's own once-a-second step, so it holds its mark steadily.

See [Flux Maintainer, Anomaly Siphon and Field Regulator](../machines/field-control.md) for the rest
of the family.
