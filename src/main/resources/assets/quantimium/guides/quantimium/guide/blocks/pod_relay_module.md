# Pod Relay Module

<ItemImage id="quantimium:pod_relay_module" scale="2" />

Part of the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md).

A <ItemLink id="quantimium:superposition_pod" /> module, set in one of the four edges of its <ItemLink id="quantimium:pod_cradle" />. It works while the pod holds one of your doubles.

Makes its pod a **hub**: it reaches every pod its Tesseracts are bound to, whether a double waits there or not. Bind pods by sneak-using one with a Tesseract and put it in the Relay (it holds six). Sends a spare double to the pod you pick, then you swap into it.

Draw: what it sends.

See the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md) page.
