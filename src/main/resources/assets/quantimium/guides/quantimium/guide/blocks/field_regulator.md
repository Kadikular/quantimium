# Field Regulator

<ItemImage id="quantimium:field_regulator" scale="2" />

The Field Regulator is a Flux Maintainer and an Anomaly Siphon in one block, with both tabs, at 80% of their FE.

- Set a flux floor and an anomaly ceiling. It works on both together, and on its own settles rather than switching on and off.
- It yields <ItemLink id="quantimium:anomaly_fragment" />s like a Siphon.
- It is the natural partner of a high-flux base: flux held where your crafts need it, anomaly kept from building up behind it.

Made from a Flux Maintainer and an Anomaly Siphon either side of a nether star, with two <ItemLink id="quantimium:tesseract" />s and four Anomaly Fragments. See [Field control](../field-control/overview.md).
