# Quantum Exciter

<ItemImage id="quantimium:quantum_exciter" scale="2" />

The Quantum Exciter turns FE into flux on purpose, to hold a chunk's field at a band. It is the early way to raise the field.

- **Yield:** 10 times as much flux per FE as ordinary work.
- **Aim:** click it to switch between Medium and High.
  - Medium: aims for 400 flux for 100 FE/t.
  - High: aims for 4,000 flux for 1,000 FE/t. A thousand FE/t holds a chunk there.
- It works flat out at no flux and eases off to nothing as the chunk reaches its aim.
- Buffer 100,000 FE, taking up to 2,000 FE/t.

The next steps up are the [Flux Maintainer and Field Regulator](../field-control/overview.md). To hold flux down instead, see the <ItemLink id="quantimium:flux_suppressor" />.
