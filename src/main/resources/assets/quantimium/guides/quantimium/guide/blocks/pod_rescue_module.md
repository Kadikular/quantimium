# Pod Rescue Module

<ItemImage id="quantimium:pod_rescue_module" scale="2" />

Part of the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md).

A <ItemLink id="quantimium:superposition_pod" /> module, set in one of the four edges of its <ItemLink id="quantimium:pod_cradle" />. It works while the pod holds one of your doubles.

Lets its pod be your **Anchor**: press **Make Anchor** on the pod's screen. When a hit would kill you, you wake in the Anchor's double instead, whole and with everything you carried. That Sophon is spent. A Totem of Undying in your hand still goes first.

Draws no power.

See the [Superposition Pod](../multiblocks/superposition-pod.md) page.
